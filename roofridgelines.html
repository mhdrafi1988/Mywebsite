Algorithmic Pillars

What RoofRidgeLines Does

Treats each selected drain / opening centroid as a Voronoi site, computes the mathematical diagram, clips it to the roof boundary, and validates equidistance before placing elements.

◆

Automatic Loop Detection

Extracts all inner loops (drains, skylights, RTU curbs) from the selected roof and classifies each as circle, rectangle, or custom shape.

▽

Voronoi Ridge Computation

Groups nearby drain seeds within a configurable proximity, then computes a Voronoi diagram on a background thread from group centroids.

✂

Boundary Clipping

Clips raw Voronoi edges to the roof's actual boundary polygon and derives ridge midpoints and shape points automatically.

✓

Equidistance Validation

Checks every ridge against a configurable tolerance and logs pass/fail per segment before any Revit elements are created.

Pipeline Architecture

Six-Step Generation Pipeline

1

Select Roof & Seeds

Pick a roof; inner loops are loaded. Tick at least 2 as drainage seeds.

2

Group Seeds

Seeds within proximity (default 50mm) are merged into drain groups.

3

Compute Voronoi

Group centroids become Voronoi sites; raw edges/vertices generated.

4

Clip to Boundary

Raw edges clipped to boundary polygon; midpoints and shape points derived.

5

Validate Equidistance

Every ridge checked against tolerance; results logged pass/fail.

6

Create Elements

Detail lines and shape points placed in active view using selected style.
